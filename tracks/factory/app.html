Factory

Droid works here as a team of custom droids: a coder, a reviewer, a credit analyst and a shopper. It plans first, and nothing runs until you approve the plan. The box enforces each task's autonomy level with its own firewall rule, budget and copy of the files. Droid never holds a key. Risky commands also pause for your OK through Droid's hooks, but that is a convenience: the hooks live in the task's own home, so the boundary is the OS (the task's own user, copy, firewall, broker and budget).
