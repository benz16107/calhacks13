Why did we decide this, and does it still hold?

The desk answers from the box's log alone: what it knew at the moment it decided, the rules it cited, who approved it and when, and anything recorded since. Ask about an application (APP-1042), a customer (C-1013) or a business name.

A credit application of your own

Type any business. The owner who signs the guarantee must be one of CRS's 20 test people: the sandbox knows only them, and no real person's credit is checked.

Or upload case files

CSV files named as in the README (customers.csv, invoices.csv, payments.csv, orders.csv, suppliers.csv, bills.csv), applications named APP-something.json, and optionally an answer_key.json to score the run. Download templates.

Every decision names the rule ids it rests on, and the desk's tools refuse a rule the file does not have. CRS and Capital One Nessie are sandboxes with test identities: no real person's credit is checked, and every payment is test money.
