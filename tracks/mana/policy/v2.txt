# Fogline Supply: who approves what. The ceiling applies first; then the first matching rule decides.
# Nothing this big is approved on the box by anyone, the owner included.
Over $50,000 can't be approved in GurtOS.
# Guests come in through the mana app; the owner answers for them, and their wording can't steer them to anyone else.
Requests from mana go to the owner.
# Fraud calls go to the fraud reviewer before any other rule can catch them.
Fraud referrals go to the fraud reviewer.
# Bank details decide where money goes; only the owner approves them, so the person who approves refunds can't also redirect them.
Bank details go to the owner.
# All other agent work is credit work; matching by app can't be steered by wording or customer names.
Requests from the credit desk go to the Controller.
# Deploys are IT's call, not finance's.
Production goes to IT.
# Write-offs from any other source (for example Sales) are the Controller's call at any size.
Write off goes to the Controller.
# Refunds send money out, so the Controller approves them at any size.
Refund goes to the Controller.
# Releasing an order on a late account is a credit decision, even when Sales asks.
Release order goes to the Controller.
# Amounts over $5,000 from any source, like Sales' one-off rush orders, are seen by finance.
Over $5,000 goes to the Controller.
# Anything unforeseen goes to the most accountable person, not whoever is free.
Everything else goes to the owner.
