# GurtOS Credit Desk

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

The credit desk of a small company running on GurtOS: a credit analyst that checks each applicant with live CRS
credit, identity and fraud checks, and a human controller who signs off on any fraud hold or large limit. It takes
work only from its own box's GurtOS agent, and sends the controller's questions back as Approve and Deny cards.
