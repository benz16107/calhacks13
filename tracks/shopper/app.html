Why GurtOS

Today's shopping agents need your card number or your store password. Stores can't tell a good agent from a bot. You can't see or stop what they buy. Cloud agents run on someone else's computer, where you don't know what happens.

GurtOS is a safe place for agents on a computer you own, even an old laptop: it is a light OS that needs 4 GB of memory. The agent never sees your card, every purchase needs your tap, the store can verify the agent, and every purchase can be undone.

Shopper is best in GurtOS Personal, on your own computer, and in GurtOS Server for routines that keep running after you close your laptop: price watching, reorders and subscription checks.

The GurtOS portal and phone app show live what every agent is doing, on your laptop or your server, and let you approve, deny, pause or undo.
