# Creative briefs: one table

Each track has its own format, shots and music bed; GurtOS fonts, colours, title card and end card stay the same. The full paragraph and footage list is the `## Creative brief` section in `tracks/<id>.md`. Slot beats (flight recorder, permissions) take the treatment of their neighbours. Zoom is used at most once a video.
